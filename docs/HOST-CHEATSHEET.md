# Throwback Throwdown: Host Cheat Sheet

You run the game from your iPhone. Patrick looks after the computer and the TV.

## 1. Get in (2 minutes)
1. On your iPhone, open **Safari** and go to **party.jpatrickbeal.com/host**
2. Type the **6-digit PIN** Patrick gives you and tap **Enter**.
3. You're in. Your screen stays awake by itself during the game.

> If it ever asks for the PIN again, just type it again. Nothing is lost.

## 2. Add the photos
1. Open the **Photo queue** section and tap **Add photos**.
2. Pick photos from your camera roll. You can select several at once. They upload one by one.
3. Use **↑ / ↓** to change the order. To remove one you added by mistake, tap **🗑** → **Yes**.

You can add more photos at any time, even in the middle of the game.

## 3. Let everyone join
- People scan the **QR code on the TV** with their phone camera and type their name.
- They're put on teams **automatically**, in the order they join, so teams stay even.
- In the **Teams** section:
  - **Move…** puts one person on a different team.
  - **🔀 Shuffle teams** mixes everyone up randomly.
- Teams can rename themselves from their phones (you can rename them too).

## 4. Run the game: just press the big button
The big button at the top always says what comes next:

| The big button says | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Start game** | The first photo flips onto the TV |
| **Start captions** | Each team writes a funny caption on their phones (75 seconds) |
| **Start voting** | Everyone votes for the funniest caption (30 seconds), but not for their own team |
| **Show results** | The TV reveals the votes and the winning team |
| **Scoreboard** | The team standings |
| **Next photo** | The next round starts |
| **Final results** | Shown when the photos run out: the podium! |
| **New game** | Starts over with scores at zero |

**Captions and voting end on their own** when everyone is done or time runs out.
You only need to press the button if you want to move on early.

While people are writing or voting, you'll see things like **"3 of 5 teams have a caption"** and **Waiting on** (who's still writing).
- **+30s** gives everyone more time.
- **Skip** ends the timer now.

## 5. If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A caption isn't family-friendly | **Captions** section → **Hide** (do it before or during voting) |
| A stranger joined, or someone joined twice | **Players** section → **Kick** → **Kick? Yes** |
| Someone is on the wrong team | **Teams** section → **Move…** next to their name |
| A score needs fixing | **Players** section → **−100** / **+100** |
| We need to stop early | **End game now** → **Yes** (jumps to the final podium) |
| Your screen says "Reconnecting…" | Wait a few seconds. If it stays stuck, pull down to refresh the page. |
| A player's phone froze or died | They open the same link on the same phone and they'll be put back on their team |
| The TV or computer has a problem | Get Patrick. The game remembers everything, even if it restarts. |

Have fun! 🎉
